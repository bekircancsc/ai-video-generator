# Please Do Not Press Four — publishing pack

Five vertical shorts, one continuous story. Payloads live beside this file as
`floor-four-part-1.json` … `-5.json`; rendered output lands in `out/` as
`<slug>.mp4` with a matching `<slug>.jpg` cover.

The series is built to be watched in order and to reward a rewatch: part 5 ends
on the line part 1 opens with. That only pays off if the parts go out in order,
spaced apart, so publish one every two days rather than all in one drop.

## Pinned comments

One per part, posted at upload and pinned. They carry the next link and give
people something to answer — a comment thread is what carries a series between
parts.

- **1** — "The fire map in my old office did this. Three, then five. Has anyone else worked in a building with no fourth floor?"
- **2** — "Count the stairs in your own building tonight. Eleven per floor is standard. Tell me what you get between three and five."
- **3** — "Twenty two years of entries and not one exit scan. What would you have done with that log?"
- **4** — "He was polite. That's the whole reason he's still there. Part 5 ends it."
- **5** — "Now go back to part 1 and listen to the first line again."

## Upload checklist

Per video, before publishing:

- Upload `out/<slug>.mp4`, set `out/<slug>.jpg` as the thumbnail.
- Title and description from the tables above.
- Add to the **Please Do Not Press Four** playlist, in order.
- Audience: not made for kids.
- Schedule for the date above at 18:00, don't publish immediately.
- Post and pin the comment once it is live.

## If you add more parts

Keep the shape: six scenes, roughly 45 seconds, one amber theme colour
(`#a8903c`), no face ever resolvable, and a last line that hands the viewer
something to do. New parts slot in before part 5 — part 5 is the ending.
